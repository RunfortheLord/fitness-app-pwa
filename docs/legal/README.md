# Vigorune Version 1 Legal Publication Set

The canonical consumer-facing documents for the approved free, U.S.-only
Version 1 release are:

- `TERMS_OF_USE.md`
- `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`
- `CONSUMER_HEALTH_DATA_PRIVACY_POLICY.md`

They are written in publication form, contain no drafting placeholders, and
describe the current product boundary: core local use without an account,
optional Apple/Google sign-in that activates automatic cloud snapshot backup,
no subscription or advertising, user-requested remote provider features, and
separate local, cloud, and account deletion.

The similarly named files containing `DRAFT` or `WORKING_DRAFT` are superseded
internal research for a possible future worldwide subscription release. They
must not be used as Version 1 public text.

## Publication controls

Before the canonical documents are made effective in a production release:

1. Have qualified U.S. counsel review the documents, especially eligibility,
   consumer-health consent, warranty and liability language, state-law scope,
   and the privacy-request procedure. Publication-form text is not a substitute
   for legal advice.
2. Publish complete, accessible `/terms`, `/privacy`, and
   `/consumer-health-data-privacy` pages and link them conspicuously from the app
   and public site. Keep `/account-deletion` and `/support` available signed out.
3. Ensure the submitted build and production configuration match every factual
   statement, including U.S.-only availability, 18+ eligibility, disabled
   analytics and feedback, optional sign-in, automatic backup after sign-in,
   OpenAI meal analysis, food providers, web push, retention, and deletion.
4. Add a clear OpenAI disclosure and required affirmative permission before a
   meal image or note is transmitted to OpenAI.
5. Resolve Sign in with Apple token revocation for account deletion or obtain a
   documented App Review determination that the implemented flow is acceptable.
6. Establish and test the privacy-request, identity-verification,
   recipient-notification, deletion, appeal, and response-tracking procedure.
7. Maintain an FTC Health Breach Notification Rule incident-response procedure
   and the contacts needed to execute it.
8. Preserve the exact published text, effective date, app version, and evidence
   of any Terms acceptance. Material changes require a new version and any
   notice or renewed agreement required by law.

Do not enable analytics, feedback, subscriptions, mandatory login, or a new
data recipient without first reconciling the product, consent flow, store
privacy answers, and these documents.
